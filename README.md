# Experimental Investigation of Newton's Law of Cooling

## Project Overview

This project investigates the cooling behaviour of a heated object under different environmental conditions, with a particular focus on comparing natural convection and forced convection.

The experiment involves collecting temperature measurements over time as the object cools under different conditions. The collected data is then processed and analysed using computational and statistical methods to understand the underlying heat transfer mechanisms.

## Objectives

- Investigate the temperature variation of a hot object with respect to time.
- Study the applicability of Newton's Law of Cooling to the experimental observations.
- Estimate cooling constants under different experimental conditions.
- Compare natural cooling with fan-assisted cooling (forced convection).
- Evaluate the influence of airflow on the rate of heat transfer.
- Apply statistical methods to determine the significance of differences in cooling behaviour.

## Methodology

The project combines experimental measurements with computational analysis:

1. **Experimental Data Collection:** Record temperature measurements at regular time intervals under different cooling conditions.
2. **Data Processing:** Organise, clean, and prepare the collected temperature-time datasets.
3. **Mathematical Modelling:** Apply Newton's Law of Cooling and exponential curve fitting to model temperature decay.
4. **Parameter Estimation:** Determine cooling constants for the different experimental conditions.
5. **Comparative Analysis:** Examine the differences in cooling rates between natural and forced convection.
6. **Statistical Analysis:** Use confidence intervals, estimated standard errors, and hypothesis testing to assess the significance of the observed differences.
7. **Visualisation:** Present temperature-time curves, fitted models, and statistical results to interpret the experimental findings.

## Key Findings

The analysis demonstrates that forced convection through the use of a fan increases the cooling constant compared to natural convection. The results highlight the influence of increased airflow on convective heat transfer and the overall cooling behaviour of the heated object.
